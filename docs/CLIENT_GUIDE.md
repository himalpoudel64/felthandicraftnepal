# Looking after your website

Everything on your website is edited in one place, called the Studio. You will get its web address and an invitation by email. Sign in with the same account each time.

On the left of the Studio you will see:

- **Products by category:** pick a category to see its products
- **All products** and **Hidden products**
- **Categories:** Ornaments, Hotpot, Keyrings and so on
- **Home Page:** the big photographs at the top, your story, your impact
- **About Page:** your story, mission, vision and how things are made
- **Site Settings:** your name, email, WhatsApp number and logo

## The most important thing to know

Nothing changes on the website until you press the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

After you publish, the website rebuilds itself. This takes a minute or two. Then refresh the page in your browser. If you publish several things in a row, that is fine.

## Add a product

1. Click **Products by category**, then the category it belongs to.
2. Click the **pencil icon** at the top.
3. Type the **Product name**, then press **Generate** next to Web address.
4. Write a **Short description** (one sentence) and a **Full description** (a few sentences). Say what it is, how it is made and who it suits.
5. Add **Tags** if you like, such as Christmas. Visitors can filter by them.
6. Open the **Photos** tab and add your photos. The first is the main one. Drag them to change the order.
7. Click each photo and fill in **Alt text**. This is a short description of the photo, such as "Red felt purse with a brass zip, held in one hand". The Studio will not let you publish without it.
8. Open the **Sizes and prices** tab and add at least one size.
9. Press **Publish**.

## Add or change a size and price

1. Open the product and click the **Sizes and prices** tab.
2. Click **Add item**, or click an existing size to change it.
3. Type the **Size**, such as Medium, and the **Price in NPR**, using numbers only, such as 650.
4. Close the small window and press **Publish**.

The first size in the list is the one shown when the page opens. Drag sizes to change the order.

**Sample prices:** every product starts with an example price of NPR 450, marked with a tick beside "These prices are only examples". When you enter the real price, **remove that tick** and press Publish. While the tick is there, the website says "sample price" and hides the price on product cards.

## Hide a product or category

Open the item. On a product, click the **Where it appears** tab. Set **Show on website?** to **Hidden**, then press **Publish**. It disappears from the website and from Google's list of your pages, but stays safe in the Studio.

Hiding a category also hides the products in it.

## Add a category

Click **Categories**, then the pencil icon. Type the name, press Generate, add an image with alt text, and set a **Display order**. Lower numbers come first. Using 10, 20, 30 makes it easy to slot one in between later, such as 15.

## Change the large photographs on the home page

1. Open **Home Page**. You will start on the **Top of page** tab.
2. Under **Slideshow images**, click a photo to replace it, or **Add item** to add another.
3. Fill in the **Alt text**.
4. Drag photos to change the order. Make the first one your best photo of someone at work, because it loads first.
5. Press **Publish**.

Use wide photos, at least 2000 pixels across. Clicking a photo's crop icon lets you mark the most important point, so it stays in view on phones.

## Replace the temporary photographs

Some photographs of women at work are **temporary stock photos of weaving at other businesses, and do not show your team**. Please replace them with your own as soon as you can, before you tell people about the website. They are on the Home Page and About Page.

To replace one, upload your photo, write the alt text, **clear the Photo credit**, **untick "This is a temporary stock photo"**, and press Publish.

The best photographs show real hands and faces at work: shaping wool, stitching, cutting, working together. Please ask each person's permission before using their photograph.

## Good to know

- **"Sample text":** anything starting with these words was put there so the website could be built. Please replace it with your own words.
- **Products starting with "Sample":** these are examples. Delete them, or edit them into real products.
- **Coloured blocks saying "Photograph coming soon":** these mean a photo has not been added yet.
- **Only write what is true.** Please do not add reviews, awards or figures you cannot back up. The **Verified figures** box on the Home Page stays hidden until you add a number you can prove.
- **Enquiries** arrive at your email address. They are not stored in the Studio. Please check your Spam folder the first time.
- **WhatsApp number:** digits only in Site Settings, starting with 977, with no plus sign or spaces.
- **Web address:** changing the address of a published product breaks links people have saved. Ask your developer first.
- **SEO fields** are optional. If you leave them empty, the website writes sensible ones for you.

If you make a mistake before pressing Publish, use **Discard changes** from the small arrow next to Publish. After publishing, the **History** icon at the top lets you go back to an earlier version.

The **View on website** button, in the same small arrow menu, opens the published page.
