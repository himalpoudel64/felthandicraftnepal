# Looking after your website

Everything on your website is edited in one place, called the Studio. You will get its web address and an invitation by email. Sign in with the same account each time.

On the left of the Studio you will see:

- **Site Settings:** your name, email, phone, WhatsApp number, address, logo
- **Home Page:** the large images at the top, featured products, impact text
- **About Page:** your story, mission, vision, process and photos
- **Categories:** Ornaments, Animals and Figures, Purse, Bags, Hats and so on
- **Products:** open a category to see the products inside it

## The most important thing to know

Nothing changes on the website until you press the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

After you publish, the website rebuilds itself. This takes a minute or two. Then refresh the page in your browser to see the change. If you publish several things in a row, that is fine.

## Add a product

1. Click **Products**, then the category it belongs to.
2. Click the **pencil icon** at the top to create a new product.
3. Type the **Name**, then press **Generate** next to Web address.
4. Add **Photos**. The first photo is the main one. Drag them to change the order.
5. Click each photo and fill in **Alt text**, which is a short description of what the photo shows, such as "Red felt purse with a brass zip, held in one hand". The Studio will not let you publish without it.
6. Write a **Short description** (one sentence) and a **Full description** (a few sentences). Say what it is, how it is made and who it suits. Very short descriptions rarely show up on Google, and the Studio will warn you.
7. Add **Specifications** if you like, such as Material and Wool felt.
8. Add at least one size. See below.
9. Press **Publish**.

## Add a size and price

1. Open the product and find **Sizes and prices**.
2. Click **Add item**.
3. Type the **Size**, such as Medium, and the **Price (NPR)**, using numbers only, such as 650.
4. Close the small window and press **Publish**.

The first size in the list is the one shown when the page opens. You can drag sizes to change their order.

## Hide a product

Open the product, set **Status** to **Hidden**, and press **Publish**. It disappears from the website, including from Google's list of your pages, but stays safe in the Studio. Set it back to **Published** whenever you want it back.

A category can be hidden in the same way. Hiding a category also hides all its products.

## Change the order

Products and categories each have a **Display order** number. Lower numbers come first. Using 10, 20, 30 makes it easy to slot something in between later, such as 15.

## Change the large images on the home page

1. Open **Home Page** and find **Hero images**.
2. Click an image to replace it, or **Add item** to add another.
3. Fill in the **Alt text**. A caption is optional.
4. Drag the images to change the order. The first one is shown first and loads fastest, so make it your best photo.
5. Press **Publish**.

Use wide photos, at least 2000 pixels across. The menu sits over the top of the first photo, so choose images that are not very pale along the top edge.

If you click the photo's crop icon you can set the most important point in the picture. The website will keep that point visible on phones.

## Good to know

- **Sample content:** anything marked "Sample" was put there so the website could be built. Please replace it all before you tell people about the site.
- **Prices:** every product shows a sample price of NPR 450 until you set the real one. Open each product, change the size name from "One size (sample price)" to the real size, type the real price, and press Publish.
- **Coloured blocks marked "Placeholder":** these mean a photo has not been added yet.
- **WhatsApp buttons** only appear once you add your number in **Site Settings**. Use digits only, starting with the country code: 977 and then your number, with no plus sign or spaces.
- **Enquiries** from the website arrive at your email address. They are not stored in the Studio.
- **Only write what is true.** Please do not add reviews, awards or figures you cannot back up.
- **SEO fields** are optional. If you leave them empty, the website writes sensible ones for you.
- **Changing a web address** of a published product will break any links people have saved. Ask your developer to add a redirect if you need to do this.

If you make a mistake before pressing Publish, use **Discard changes** from the small arrow next to Publish. After publishing, the **History** icon at the top lets you go back to an earlier version.
